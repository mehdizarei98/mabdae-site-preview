About Mabda

A legal matter often starts with questions. Our approach begins with listening to your account and reading the documents before discussing the next steps.

Our approach

Mabda is based in Tehran.

We aim to explain legal terms in everyday language. A discussion of your circumstances and documents helps identify the questions that need closer review.

The scope of any work and the terms of engagement should be agreed in writing. An introductory conversation does not guarantee an outcome.

Working principles

  • Listening

    Begin with the circumstances and the documents that explain them.

  • Clear language

    Explain the questions and possible next steps in plain language.

  • Written scope

    Set out the work and terms of engagement in writing.

  • Care with information

    Discuss how documents and personal information will be handled.

Before choosing a lawyer

Check a lawyer's licence details with the issuing Bar Association before engaging them.
